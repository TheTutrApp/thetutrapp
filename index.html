FAQ

What people ask us.

When will I get access?

We invite 14 new teams a week, roughly in the order of joining. Expect 4–10 weeks from signup to invite. If you're Series-A-or-later and ready to replace a full finance stack, write to nat@northbeam.co — we front-load that cohort.

How much does it cost?

$540/mo flat for up to 50 FTEs, $980/mo up to 200. No seats-based pricing. No per-integration charges. Beta pricing is locked for life if you join before GA.

Is my banking data safe?

Read-only Plaid tokens only. SOC 2 Type II (Oct 2025). All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We host on AWS us-east-1, with optional eu-west-1 for EU companies.

Do you replace QuickBooks?

No. Northbeam sits alongside your accounting system. We read from it; your bookkeeper still owns the books. The two systems agree to the penny because we reconcile nightly.

Who's not a good fit?

Pre-seed teams (you don't need us yet) and any company over 300 FTEs (use Pigment or Anaplan). Also agencies and consultancies — our revenue model doesn't suit services businesses.
